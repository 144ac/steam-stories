Why must the string be tight?

When you whisper into the cup, the bottom of the cup wobbles — tiny, tiny wobbles called VIBRATIONS.

A TIGHT string is like a solid little bridge — the vibrations run straight along it, all the way to the other cup, and your friend hears your voice.

A LOOSE string just flops about. The wobbles sink into the droop and never arrive.

This is how sound moves through anything solid — by passing tiny vibrations along, from one tiny piece to the next.
